## Common questions

### How do I set up the AI reflections?

Pathoragy uses a **bring-your-own-key** model: you connect your own AI provider so your data goes directly from your device to that provider, never through us.

1. Open **Settings** in the app.
2. Choose a provider — **DeepSeek** (default), **OpenAI**, **Cloudflare Workers AI**, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
3. Paste your **API key** from that provider.
4. Tap **"Generate this week's reflection"** when you want a reflection.

You can switch providers or clear your key any time in Settings.

### Why isn't anything happening when I tap "Generate"?

The AI features only work once you've added a provider and API key in **Settings** (see above). If you've added one and it still fails, double-check that the key is valid and has credit/quota with your provider.

### How do I verify a Waypoint with a photo?

Tap **"Verify with photo,"** take a photo **in the moment**, and Pathoragy sends that single image to your configured AI Vision provider for verification. Pathoragy never reads your existing photo library — only the live shot you take. You can decline camera access in iOS Settings if you'd rather not use this feature.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID, so you manage them in iOS:

**Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Pathoragy**

There you can change plan or cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid period. For refund requests, use Apple's process at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.

### Where is my data stored? Can I delete it?

Your Logbook, scores, and Boat live **on your device** (and, if you enabled it, your private iCloud) — not on our servers. To delete everything, **delete the app** (and remove the iCloud copy in iOS Settings if you used one). See the [Privacy Policy](/pathoragy/privacy) for the full picture.

### I restored my device / got a new phone — where's my data?

If you had iCloud enabled for Pathoragy, signing into the same Apple ID and reinstalling restores your data. If you did not use iCloud, local-only data does not transfer between devices.
